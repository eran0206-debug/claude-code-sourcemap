# Ahmad Caver — Game Day Vlog

One game night becomes one film. We introduce Ahmad through a game day first, then let people meet the person behind the player. This page is what we are asking you to approve: the idea, the story, and how the day will work.

## The idea

**We are not filming a basketball game. We are introducing Ahmad Caver through a basketball game.**

The game is the way in. People already know you can play. What they do not know yet is who you are at 4pm on game day, what you think about in the tunnel, and who you are when you leave the arena. This film gives them the player first, then the person, so they have a reason to follow you after the final buzzer.

**The feel:** natural, cinematic, personal, confident. Not over-produced. Not a highlight tape.

**Three rules we will keep:**

1. Your voice tells the story. No narrator, no studio interview, no text walls. Your own words from a relaxed conversation run over the whole film.
2. Real beats staged ones. We follow your actual routine. We never ask you to fake a moment or repeat something for the camera.
3. The game is the peak, not the point. The game gets about a minute and a half of a five-minute film. The rest is you.

## The story in seven beats

The film opens near the end, with one line from you over a quiet arena, then jumps back to the start of your day. From there it runs in order to the final buzzer and beyond.

## Your game day

We fit around you, not the other way around. Your routine and your performance come first, always.

- **Before the game:** a two-person crew meets you at arrival. One short question on the walk in. After that we are invisible until the buzzer.
- **During the game:** nothing. We shoot from the baseline and the stands.
- **Right after:** five minutes in the tunnel or locker room for your raw reaction, before the showers and the phone.
- **Later that night or the next day:** 30 to 40 minutes of relaxed conversation somewhere that feels like you. We talk, you answer. No script, no rehearsed lines.
- **Wardrobe:** wear what you would wear anyway. Clean, simple, no busy logos that are not yours. Bring your real headphones and your real bag.
- **Off limits:** tell us anything you do not want on camera, people or topics, and it stays out. Final cut comes to you before anything is posted.
