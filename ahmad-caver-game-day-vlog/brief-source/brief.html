Part 1

Arrival / Hook

Goal
Pull the viewer into Ahmad’s day immediately.
Capture
  • Ahmad arriving at the arena
  • Walking toward / into the venue
  • Shoes / bag / headphones / details
  • Natural interactions
  • 1–2 quick lines to camera before the game
Simple prompt
“What’s the mindset going into tonight?”
Opening style
Quick cinematic shots + Ahmad’s voice over them.
Part 2

Pre-game

Goal
Build anticipation.
Capture
  • Stepping onto the court
  • Warmups
  • Shooting routine
  • Stretching / preparation
  • Teammate interactions
  • Quiet focused moments
  • Close-ups + wide arena shots
Story
The energy slowly builds toward tip-off.

Part 3

The Game

Goal
Show Ahmad as the athlete.
Capture
  • Best plays
  • Reactions
  • Defense / offense
  • Bench moments
  • Crowd / atmosphere
  • Scoreboard / game details
  • Emotional moments
Edit
Do not make this the entire vlog. Use the game as the peak of the story.
Part 4

After the Game

Goal
Transition from basketball player → person.
First, let Ahmad react naturally to the game.
Questions
  • “How did that one feel?”
  • “What stood out to you tonight?”
  • “What’s the mindset after a game like this?”
Keep answers natural and short.

Part 5

Meet Ahmad

Goal
Give viewers a reason to follow beyond tonight.
More relaxed, off-court conversation.
Topics
  • Who is Ahmad Caver?
  • Where is he from?
  • How long has basketball been part of his life?
  • What keeps him hungry?
  • What does he still enjoy about the game?
  • What does he enjoy outside basketball?
  • What side of himself does he want people to see?
  • What can people expect from his future content?
Important
This should feel like a conversation — not an interview.
Part 6

Ending / Next Chapter

Finish with a short statement from Ahmad that creates continuation.
Example direction
“This is only the beginning. You’ll see a lot more this season.”
Then
  • Final cinematic shots
  • Walk away / arena exit
  • EA Visuals × Ahmad Caver
