Game Day Vlog — First Chapter

Arrival
We’ll get there at 6:30 PM
Where
Ramat Gan · Home game
Outputs
YouTube vlog · Instagram Reel

Core Idea

A short cinematic game-day story that introduces Ahmad through basketball first — then lets people meet the person behind the player.

Feel

Natural · Cinematic · Personal · Confident

Not over-produced. Not a generic highlight video.

Part 1

Arrival / Hook

Goal
Pull the viewer into Ahmad’s day immediately.
Capture
Ahmad arriving at the arena·Walking toward / into the venue·Shoes / bag / headphones / details·Natural interactions·1–2 quick lines to camera before the game
Prompt
“What’s the mindset going into tonight?”
Opening
Quick cinematic shots + Ahmad’s voice over them.
Part 2

Pre-game

Goal
Build anticipation.
Capture
Stepping onto the court·Warmups·Shooting routine·Stretching / preparation·Teammate interactions·Quiet focused moments·Close-ups + wide arena shots
Story
The energy slowly builds toward tip-off.
Part 3

The Game

Goal
Show Ahmad as the athlete.
Capture
Best plays·Reactions·Defense / offense·Bench moments·Crowd / atmosphere·Scoreboard / game details·Emotional moments
Edit
Do not make this the entire vlog. Use the game as the peak of the story.
Part 4

After the Game

Goal
Transition from basketball player → person. First, let Ahmad react naturally to the game.
Questions
“How did that one feel?”·“What stood out to you tonight?”·“What’s the mindset after a game like this?”
Keep answers natural and short.
Part 5

Meet Ahmad

Goal
Give viewers a reason to follow beyond tonight. More relaxed, off-court conversation.
Topics
Who is Ahmad Caver?·Where is he from?·How long has basketball been part of his life?·What keeps him hungry?·What does he still enjoy about the game?·What does he enjoy outside basketball?·What side of himself does he want people to see?·What can people expect from his future content?
Important
This should feel like a conversation — not an interview.
Part 6

Ending / Next Chapter

Finish with a short statement from Ahmad that creates continuation.
Direction
“This is only the beginning. You’ll see a lot more this season.”
Then
Final cinematic shots·Walk away / arena exit·E.A. Visuals × Ahmad Caver

YouTube VlogTarget: 4–7 minutes

Flow: Arrival → Pre-game → Warmups → Game → Post-game → Personal Intro → What’s Next

Instagram ReelTarget: 30–60 seconds

Fast cinematic version of the same story: Arrival → Preparation → Best Game Moments → Ahmad Personality → Strong Closing Line

The Main Rule
We’re not filming a basketball game. We’re introducing Ahmad Caver through a basketball game.
