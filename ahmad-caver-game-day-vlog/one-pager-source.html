We’re not filming a basketball game.
We’re introducing Ahmad Caver through a basketball game.
Core idea

A short cinematic game-day story that introduces Ahmad through basketball first — then lets people meet the person behind the player.

Feel

Natural · Cinematic · Personal · Confident

Not over-produced. Not a generic highlight video.

1Arrival / Hook

Goal: Pull the viewer into Ahmad’s day immediately.

Capture: Ahmad arriving at the arena · Walking toward / into the venue · Shoes / bag / headphones / details · Natural interactions · 1–2 quick lines to camera before the game

Simple prompt: “What’s the mindset going into tonight?”

Opening style: Quick cinematic shots + Ahmad’s voice over them.

2Pre-game

Goal: Build anticipation.

Capture: Stepping onto the court · Warmups · Shooting routine · Stretching / preparation · Teammate interactions · Quiet focused moments · Close-ups + wide arena shots

Story: The energy slowly builds toward tip-off.

3The Game

Goal: Show Ahmad as the athlete.

Capture: Best plays · Reactions · Defense / offense · Bench moments · Crowd / atmosphere · Scoreboard / game details · Emotional moments

Edit: Do not make this the entire vlog. Use the game as the peak of the story.

4After the Game

Goal: Transition from basketball player → person.

First, let Ahmad react naturally to the game.

Questions: “How did that one feel?” · “What stood out to you tonight?” · “What’s the mindset after a game like this?”

Keep answers natural and short.

5Meet Ahmad

Goal: Give viewers a reason to follow beyond tonight.

More relaxed, off-court conversation.

Topics:

  • Who is Ahmad Caver?
  • Where is he from?
  • How long has basketball been part of his life?
  • What keeps him hungry?
  • What does he still enjoy about the game?
  • What does he enjoy outside basketball?
  • What side of himself does he want people to see?
  • What can people expect from his future content?

Important: This should feel like a conversation — not an interview.

6Ending / Next Chapter

Finish with a short statement from Ahmad that creates continuation.

Example direction: “This is only the beginning. You’ll see a lot more this season.”

Then: Final cinematic shots · Walk away / arena exit · EA Visuals × Ahmad Caver

YouTube Vlog Target: 4–7 minutes

Flow: Arrival → Pre-game → Warmups → Game → Post-game → Personal Intro → What’s Next

Instagram Reel Target: 30–60 seconds

Fast cinematic version of the same story: Arrival → Preparation → Best Game Moments → Ahmad Personality → Strong Closing Line
