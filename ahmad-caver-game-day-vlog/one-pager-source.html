GAME DAY

Chapter One. The script and shoot plan for your first game-day film: one home night in Ramat Gan, 4 to 7 minutes on YouTube and a 45-second Reel. Read it, tick what works, and we lock the date.
We're not filming a basketball game.
We're introducing you through a basketball game.
People already know you can play. This film gives them the player first, then the person, so they have a reason to follow you after the final buzzer. Natural, cinematic, personal, confident. Not a highlight tape.

The story in seven beats

0Cold openEmpty court, lights half down. One sentence from you about who you are. Then we jump back to the start of your day.Nothing extra. The line comes from our conversation.
1ArrivalOutside the arena and the walk in: bag, shoes, headphones, your first look at the court.Arrive as you always do. One honest answer to "What's the mindset tonight?"
2Pre-gameWarm-ups, your shooting routine, stretching, the huddle, the quiet minute before tip-off.Tell us two or three real rituals so we're in place when they happen.
3The gameYour best plays, defense, reactions, the crowd. One signature moment slowed down with the sound pulled out.Play. Forget we're there.
4After the gameYour first raw reaction on the court, minutes after the buzzer. Sweat still on.Three short answers. No repeats, no script.
5Meet AhmadSomewhere that's yours: the car ride home, a late-night spot in Ramat Gan, your place. A relaxed talk, not an interview.30 to 40 minutes with us. Topics in advance, never the lines.
6Next chapterYour closing line, the arena emptying, the walk away. Card: Chapter One. EA Visuals × Ahmad Caver.One sentence that promises more. In your words.

Three promises

  • Your voice only. No narrator, no studio interview. Your words carry the whole film.
  • Nothing staged. We follow your real routine. We never ask you to repeat a moment.
  • The game is the peak, not the point. About 90 seconds of a 6-minute film. The rest is you.

Your game day

  • Before: one person, one camera, meets you at arrival. One question on the walk in, then invisible.
  • During: nothing. We shoot from the stands.
  • After: five minutes on the court, then the conversation at a place and time you choose.

Good to know

  • Wear what you'd wear anyway. Your real bag, your real headphones.
  • Off limits: any person or topic you name stays out.
  • You approve the final cut before anything is posted. Rough cut in 7 days, final in 14.
